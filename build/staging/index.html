What is the Diagnostic Test?

Before we begin teaching, we need to understand where your child stands. The diagnostic test is a 60-minute assessment that helps us identify:

  • Current understanding of concepts
  • Gaps in foundational knowledge
  • Learning patterns and pace
  • Areas that need immediate attention

This is not a pass/fail test. It's a detailed evaluation that helps us create the right learning plan for your child.

What happens after the test?

After the assessment, we'll have a detailed discussion with you about:

  • Your child's current level
  • Specific gaps we identified
  • Our recommended plan — batch size, number of classes per week, and timing
  • How we'll approach the teaching

This conversation helps you decide if we're the right fit before committing to regular classes.
