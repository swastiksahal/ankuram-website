Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ankuram ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit ankuramtuition.com or contact us about Class 8 tuition services in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide — name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email, and any details you share when you call or message us (e.g. your child's school, board, class).
  • Automatically collected data — basic analytics including IP address, device type, browser, referring page, and Google Ads click identifiers (gclid). This is collected via Google Tag (gtag.js) and Google Analytics.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — used by Google for ad measurement and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

2. How we use the information

  • To respond to your enquiry and book the Diagnostic Assessment.
  • To communicate with you about your child's progress (if enrolled).
  • To measure the performance of our Google Ads campaigns (e.g. which keyword led to your enquiry).
  • To improve the website and our services.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Google — for ad measurement and analytics (anonymised where possible).
  • Service providers — hosting and email delivery vendors who are contractually bound to confidentiality.
  • When required by law — to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data retention

We retain enquiry records for up to 24 months for follow-up and analytics purposes. Enrolled student data is retained for the duration of the engagement plus 12 months. You can request deletion at any time by emailing us.

5. Your rights

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

6. Children's privacy

This website is intended for parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children under 18. Student information is provided by the parent or guardian during enrolment.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (HTTPS, access controls) to protect your information. No internet transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
