Frequently Asked Questions

How does online class 10 tuition work?

Live 60-minute sessions with 3–5 students. Students solve problems, photograph their work, share on WhatsApp, and get real-time corrections from the teacher.

What subjects do you teach?

Maths is the primary subject. Science is available as an optional add-on. All major boards supported: CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, State Board.

What are the fees for online class 10 tuition?

Maths (CBSE and ICSE): ₹10,000/month for 3 sessions a week, ₹12,000 for 4, ₹15,000 for 5. IGCSE maths: ₹10,000 / ₹13,000 / ₹16,000. Maths + Science together: ₹16,000/month at any frequency. Diagnostic assessment: ₹750 for CBSE and ICSE, ₹1,000 for IGCSE.

Is online tuition effective for board exams?

Yes. Small batches of 3–5 students ensure active participation. Real-time corrections via WhatsApp photos and board-specific preparation make online tuition highly effective.

Can students outside Hyderabad join?

Yes. Students can join from anywhere in India. All you need is a phone or laptop, a notebook, and an internet connection.

How is ANKURAM different from large online platforms?

We teach 3–5 students per batch, not 15–50+. Every student actively solves in class. Corrections happen in real time, not after homework submission.

What if my child is very weak in maths?

We start with a diagnostic test to find exact gaps. If needed, we fix foundations from Class 7–8 level before moving to Class 10 topics.

What technology is needed?

A phone or laptop, a notebook, and WhatsApp. No special apps or expensive equipment required.

What are the class timings?

Most batches run between 4–9 PM IST. Exact timings are decided after the diagnostic test based on student availability.

Is there a trial class?

The diagnostic test (from ₹750) is more useful than a trial class — it tells us exactly where your child stands and helps us build the right learning plan.
