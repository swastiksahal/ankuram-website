Maths Tuition in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad (Grades 1–12)

ANKURAM Tuition Centre offers expert maths tuition in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad for Grades 1-12 across all curricula. Our small-batch approach (3-5 students) ensures strong foundations, regular practice, and visible improvement. Whether your child needs help with basics or advanced problem-solving, we build confidence step by step.

What makes our maths tuition effective

Foundation-first approach

We diagnose gaps early and strengthen fundamentals before moving to advanced topics

Step-by-step problem solving

Students learn correct methods, avoid common mistakes, and build problem-solving confidence

Regular practice & revision

Mixed practice keeps old topics active while building new skills systematically

Personal attention

Small batches mean every student gets individual feedback and faster doubt clearing

Maths tuition by grade

Grades 1–5 (Building Basics)

  • Number sense, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
  • Word problems and practical applications
  • Daily homework support and practice habits

Grades 6–10 (Concept Mastery)

  • Algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry
  • Application-based problems and exam-style questions
  • Time management and test readiness

Grades 11–12 (Advanced Problem Solving)

  • Calculus, coordinate geometry, probability, statistics
  • Board exam preparation and strategy
  • Error tracking and targeted improvement

All curricula supported

Our maths tuition aligns with curriculum requirements:

  • CBSE: NCERT-aligned, step marking focus
  • ICSE/ISC: Deep explanation, strong presentation
  • IGCSE: Concept + application, command words
  • IB MYP/DP: Criterion-based, clear communication
  • A Levels: Method marks, rigorous practice
  • State Board: Clear basics, Telugu medium available

Small batches (3–5 students)

Our batches are intentionally small so every student gets:

  • More guided practice in class
  • Faster doubt clearing
  • Better accountability and consistency
  • Personalized feedback on errors
