IB PYP Programme (Class 1-5)

The IB Primary Years Programme focuses on inquiry-based learning, helping young students develop conceptual understanding through exploration and discovery.

Inquiry-Based Maths

Students explore mathematical concepts through questions, investigations, and real-world connections. Learning is meaningful and engaging.

Conceptual Foundation

Building strong mathematical foundations that prepare students for IB MYP. Understanding 'why' and 'how', not just 'what'.

Small Batches

Personalized attention in batches of 3-5 students ensures every child's questions are answered and learning is tailored to their pace.

Learn More About IB PYP Tuition

IB MYP Programme (Class 6-10)

The IB Middle Years Programme emphasizes criterion-based assessment, inquiry, and real-world applications.

Maths & Physics Coaching

Comprehensive coaching covering Number, Algebra, Geometry, Statistics for maths, and Physics concepts aligned with MYP framework.

Criterion-Based Assessment

Preparation for all four criteria: Knowing and Understanding, Investigating Patterns, Communicating, and Applying Mathematics.

eAssessment Support

Preparation for IB MYP eAssessments with past paper practice and exam technique training.

Learn More About IB MYP Tuition

IB DP Programme (Class 11-12)

The IB Diploma Programme offers rigorous academic preparation with Internal Assessments and Extended Essay requirements.

Math AA & Math AI

Expert coaching for both Math AA (Analysis and Approaches) and Math AI (Applications and Interpretation) at HL and SL levels. We help students choose the right option based on their interests.

Physics HL/SL

Comprehensive Physics coaching covering all topics with emphasis on conceptual clarity, mathematical applications, and exam preparation.

IA & EE Support

Guidance for Internal Assessments (IA) and Extended Essay (EE) including topic selection, methodology, data analysis, and presentation.
