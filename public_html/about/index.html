Small-batch teaching for students who need clarity, not crowding

Ankuram Tuition Centre is a small-batch tutoring centre in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad. We teach Mathematics and Science to students in Classes 6 through 12 across CBSE, ICSE, IB MYP/DP, IGCSE, and Cambridge A-Levels. Every student starts with a diagnostic test. Every batch has a maximum of 3–5 students. The founder teaches every class personally.

Meet Swastik Sahal

MSc Physics | BE Mechanical Engineering | Ex-Amazon Software Engineer | 13+ Years Teaching

Why Swastik Left Amazon to Teach

I worked as a software engineer at Amazon. The pay was good, but I kept meeting students — friends' siblings, neighbours' kids — who were failing not because they lacked intelligence but because they had gaps nobody had identified. A student struggling with Trigonometry in Class 10 almost always had an unresolved gap in Algebra from Class 7 or 8. No school and no tuition centre was checking for this. I started Ankuram to fix that. Every student takes a diagnostic before I teach them anything. And I teach every batch myself — no TAs, no recorded lectures, no rotating tutors.

The Ankuram Approach

  • Diagnostic before enrollment — ₹750 assessment finds the real gaps
  • Small batches of 3–5 — not 25 or 50
  • Founder teaches every class — no rotating tutors
  • Hybrid model — online convenience + in-person accountability
  • Foundation before advancement — we fix Class 7 gaps before teaching Class 10 topics
