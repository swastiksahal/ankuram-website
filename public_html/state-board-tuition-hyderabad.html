Exam pattern: two papers + internal assessment

The TS SSC Maths exam is divided into Paper I and Paper II, with 50 marks per paper: 40 marks for theory and 10 marks for internal assessment per paper.

Theory marks4040

A student can lose a full grade not because they "don't understand," but because they cannot finish the paper in time, they do not present steps clearly, or they are inconsistent in internal assessment tasks. Recent Telangana SSC results reported a 92.78% pass rate in 2025 and around 91.31% in 2024 — but a high pass rate does not answer the parent question: "Will my child score high enough to choose the next step confidently?" That requires targeted practice on high-yield chapters and exam writing.

CBSE vs State Board — what parents need to know

Choosing between CBSE and the Telangana State Board (TSBIE) is a big decision. CBSE is standardized across India, focusing on conceptual clarity and aligning with national exams like JEE and NEET. The Telangana State Board has a rigorous syllabus that covers considerable depth, but teaching in many local schools often leans toward rote learning rather than practical application. If you are considering CBSE tuition, here is how the two boards compare.

Bottom line for parents: CBSE offers wider national mobility, while State Board aligns well with state-level entrance exams like EAMCET. Regardless of the board, the goal at Ankuram is to ensure students build deep problem-solving skills they need to succeed.

Telugu medium vs English medium

If your child is Telugu medium (or bilingual at home), they often have the maths ability but struggle with specific aspects of English-medium examination.

  • Word-problem comprehension speed: Students can solve the maths but lose time decoding the English phrasing of the question. This is a language issue, not a maths issue.
  • Translating statements into equations: Converting a word problem into algebraic form requires fluency in both the language and the mathematical notation. Students who think in Telugu often add an extra mental translation step that slows them down.
  • Writing steps that earn marks: Even when the calculation is correct, unclear or incomplete step presentation costs marks in the theory section.

My approach: we do language-light maths practice first (pure calculation and structure), then shift to language-heavy word problems with clear templates. This builds confidence without overwhelming the student.

EAPCET alignment (what students should know early)

If the student's longer-term plan includes engineering or medical admissions, the exam logic changes at Intermediate level. The TG EAPCET-2026 instruction booklet describes a 3-hour (180 minutes) computer-based test with 160 multiple-choice questions: 80 Maths + 40 Physics + 40 Chemistry, each question carrying one mark. Eligibility is tied to the Intermediate (10+2) pattern with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.

So for a Class 9-10 student, the right question is: "Are we building the algebra and trigonometry base that makes Intermediate + EAPCET manageable?" That is where I focus: foundations now, reduced panic later.

Is TS SSC Maths one paper or two papers?

It is typically described as Paper I and Paper II, with marks split across the two papers and internal assessment included alongside theory marks — 50 marks per paper total.

Yes. We build bilingual comprehension systematically: first maths structure and pure calculation, then word-problem translation speed and exam writing in English.
