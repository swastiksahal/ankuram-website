What is IB PYP?

The IB Primary Years Programme (PYP) is designed for students aged 3-12 (typically Class 1-5). It focuses on developing the whole child as an inquirer, both in the classroom and in the world outside. IB PYP emphasizes inquiry-based learning, where students explore concepts through questions, investigations, and real-world connections.

Inquiry-Based Learning

Students learn by asking questions, exploring, and discovering concepts rather than memorizing facts. This builds genuine understanding and curiosity.

Conceptual Understanding

Focus on understanding 'why' and 'how' rather than just 'what'. Students develop mathematical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Real-World Connections

Maths is connected to real-life situations, making learning meaningful and relevant to young learners.

Foundation for MYP

Strong IB PYP foundations prepare students well for transitioning to IB MYP (Middle Years Programme) in Class 6.

IB PYP Maths by Grade

Class 1-2 (Ages 6-7)

  • Number recognition and counting
  • Basic operations (addition, subtraction)
  • Shapes and patterns
  • Measurement basics
  • Data handling introduction

Class 3-4 (Ages 8-9)

  • Place value and number operations
  • Multiplication and division concepts
  • Fractions introduction
  • Geometry and spatial understanding
  • Problem-solving strategies

Class 5 (Ages 10-11)

  • Advanced number operations
  • Decimals and percentages
  • Geometry concepts
  • Data handling and statistics
  • Preparation for IB MYP transition
