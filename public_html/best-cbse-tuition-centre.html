CBSE Maths & Science Tuition — Class 6 to 12

I cover CBSE Maths for every grade from Class 6 through Class 12. Science (combined Physics, Chemistry, Biology) up to Class 10 under code 086. Physics separately for Classes 11 and 12 when students need focused board preparation or JEE-aligned problem practice alongside NCERT.

The pain points I see most often are predictable. Algebra gaps from Class 7–8 surface brutally in Class 10 when polynomials and quadratics arrive together. Trigonometry becomes a speed problem because CBSE Standard (041) does not allow calculators — students who rely on one at home freeze in the exam hall. In Science, students memorise reactions but fail assertion-reason and case-study formats that the 2025–26 CBSE paper pattern now weights heavily.

Unit-wise, Algebra carries 20 of 80 marks in Class 10 Maths; Chemical Substances and World of Living are 25 marks each in Science. I structure my batches around these weights, not chapter sequence alone. If your child needs dedicated arithmetic and algebra support outside board years, our maths tuition in Jubilee Hills programme covers the same small-batch model for all grades.

For Class 12, Physics numericals with proper sign conventions and unit discipline separate 70-mark scorers from 90-mark scorers. I train step-marking habits from day one — CBSE awards method marks even when the final answer is wrong, but only if the working is visible and logical.
