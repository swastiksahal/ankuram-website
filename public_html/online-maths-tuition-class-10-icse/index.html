Choosing help in a board year

ICSE Maths does not forgive half-formed working.

Why more ICSE worksheets do not always move marks

ICSE Maths rewards structured working, commercial applications, geometry reasoning, and clean presentation. A child can know the formula and still lose marks step by step.

Toppers prove possibility. They do not always prove fit for the average struggling student.

Why more attention alone is not enough

The home-tutor instinct is correct. One-to-one attention helps only when the teacher knows what to look for, what to test, and what to repair first.

The problem is not attention alone. The problem is accurate diagnosis plus skilled correction.
